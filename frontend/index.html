AI feedback analysis

Turn messy customer feedback into clear product priorities

Rivyu pulls in app reviews, Reddit posts and support logs, groups them into themes, flags the risks that matter, and answers questions about it in plain English.

Connect your own data

The demo loads 30 sample reviews and runs the full analysis. No sign-up.

How it works

01Connect sources

Pull Google Play reviews or Reddit posts, upload a CSV of support tickets, or start with the demo dataset.

02Analyze with AI

Each item is classified by topic, sentiment, urgency and business risk, then grouped into themes with trends.

03Act on insights

Review priority alerts with suggested actions, dig into the evidence, and ask follow-up questions.
